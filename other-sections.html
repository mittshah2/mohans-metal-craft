Specialized Sections

Beyond architectural and industrial mainstays, Mohans Metal Craft provides a diverse range of specialized aluminium profiles for niche applications. Our versatile section designs provide the perfect solution for dynamic and high-mobility structures.

From high-strength bus body frames to rapid-deployment exhibition and tent structures, we supply precision-engineered sections that focus on lightweight durability and ease of assembly. With 50+ years in the industry, we offer these specialized solutions with the best competitive pricing and high-speed dispatch throughout Gujarat.
