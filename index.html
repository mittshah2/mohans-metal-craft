Our Legacy

Pioneers in wholesale Aluminium Sections in Gujarat.

A Tradition of Excellence

Mohans Metal Craft has been a cornerstone in the wholesale business of Aluminium Sections in Gujarat since 1972. We have proudly supplied to prestigious Central & State Government projects, Hospitals, Multiplexes, Cinemas, Malls, and Corporate Houses.

We provide Aluminium Sections in various premium finishes, including powder coating and colour anodizing, ensuring durability, aesthetic appeal, and structural integrity for every project.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and innovative partner in architectural aluminium solutions across India, continuously setting new standards in quality and service.

Our Mission

To deliver superior quality aluminium sections with precision and excellence, empowering architects and builders to create enduring structures while maintaining our multi-generational legacy of integrity.
