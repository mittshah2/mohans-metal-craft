Our Legacy

For over 50 years, Mohans Metal Craft has been a distinguished leader in the wholesale distribution of premium Aluminium Sections across Gujarat. Since our founding in 1972, we have remained deeply committed to providing high-quality aluminium solutions that meet the rigorous demands of modern architecture and construction.

We specialize in offering a vast range of sections with specialized finishes—including professional-grade powder coating and sophisticated colour anodizing—ensuring that every product we supply delivers exceptional durability, structural integrity, and a refined aesthetic finish.

Our multi-generational legacy of trust and reliability has made us a preferred partner for several of the region's most significant developments. We have proudly supplied to prestigious Central & State Government projects, Hospitals, Multiplexes, Cinemas, Corporate Houses, and high-end residential projects.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and innovative partner in architectural aluminium solutions across India, continuously setting new standards in quality and service.

Our Mission

To deliver superior quality aluminium sections with precision and excellence, empowering architects and builders to create enduring structures while maintaining our multi-generational legacy of integrity.

Meet Our Leadership

Harshad Shah

Founder

Mr. Harshad Shah established Mohans Metal Craft in 1972, driven by a profound vision to revolutionize the aluminium sections market in Gujarat. Under his pioneering guidance, the company established a robust foundation built upon unwavering trust, transparent business practices, and an uncompromising commitment to quality. His relentless pursuit of excellence and deep industry knowledge have cemented our reputation as a highly dependable partner for decades.

Darshan Shah

Managing Partner

Mr. Darshan Shah, the son of founder Harshad Shah, continues the prestigious legacy by propelling the business into the modern era of architecture. Recognizing the evolving demands of contemporary construction, he has successfully expanded our business footprint, introduced cutting-edge premium finishes, and diversified our catalog. Through his dynamic and forward-thinking leadership, Mohans Metal Craft continues to secure monumental projects while retaining the core values established by his father.
